Our Story

American Indian Roots, American Impact

As Indian Americans, our team is proud to bring together the cultures, experiences, and values that shape who we are. Being part of the Indian American community has taught us the importance of education, hard work, and giving back. Through Prowler Racing, we hope to use our passion for STEM to inspire and support communities across the United States. We want to show that STEM is not only about building cars—it is about building opportunities, creating connections, and making a positive impact wherever we can.

Our Name

Inspired by the Bengal Tiger

Our team, Prowler Racing, takes its name and spirit from one of the most powerful symbols of India, the Bengal tiger. These incredible animals represent strength, resilience, and determination. All of these are qualities that inspire us both on and off the track. However, Bengal tigers face serious threats to their survival, including habitat loss and human-wildlife conflict. By carrying the tiger's name, we hope to raise awareness about their conservation and support efforts to protect them. Through fundraising and community outreach, Prowler Racing aims to turn our passion for racing into action for the future of these remarkable animals.
